Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Follow the Powder is a free service that helps skiers decide where to ski, what conditions to expect, and what to wear — before leaving home. It is built by MadeForMyKids. This policy explains what information the service collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at admin@followthepowder.com.

In short: we do not have user accounts, we do not ask for your name or email address, and we do not sell anything. The service uses Google Analytics to understand how it is used, keeps its own anonymous daily counts, and stores your preferences on your own device.

1. Information we collect

Analytics (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics)

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. They record events such as pages and resorts viewed, tabs changed, forecast and what-to-wear sections opened, outbound links clicked, searches (only that a search happened — never the search text), and favorites added or removed. Google sets cookies in your browser for this. These events never include search text, anything you type, addresses, coordinates, or city names — only resort names and interface labels.

Our own anonymous daily counts

We also keep our own usage counts on our own server. Your browser sends a small anonymous signal (event type and a label, such as a resort name or a language choice) to our server, which adds one to a daily total for that event. There are no cookies and no identifiers attached, and nothing outside a fixed list of event types is ever stored. The stored data is only aggregate numbers per day — for example, how many times a resort was selected today — with no record of who did it. A short-lived, in-memory rate limit protects the counter from abuse; it is discarded every minute and is never written to storage.

Location

To measure distances and drive times, the service needs a starting point. It works in two steps, both in your browser:

  • Approximate location from your network. When the site opens, our server reads the approximate location Cloudflare already knows from your connection (city and region). Nothing about this lookup is logged or stored on our servers.
  • Refined location from your browser, only if you allow it. Your browser may ask for your precise location. We ask at most once when the site opens; if you decline or dismiss it, we keep the approximate location and never ask again. You can also pick an airport or a place yourself instead.

Your location stays in your browser. It is rounded before use, it is never stored on our servers, and it is never sent to analytics. Only the type of starting point (network, browser, or a place you picked) is recorded in the anonymous counts above.

Information stored on your device

The service saves preferences and history in your browser's local storage, so they are there when you come back. This includes your language and units, the last resort you viewed, whether you prefer summit or base conditions, your packing checklist (including items you add yourself), your favorite resorts, recently viewed resorts, your saved starting point, and cached drive routes (kept for up to 3 days). All of this stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

2. Information we do not collect

There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no payments, so we do not ask for — and do not receive — your name, email address, password, or payment details. The only way to contact us is by emailing admin@followthepowder.com; if you do, we receive whatever you choose to send in that email. The text you type into the site's search box and packing list stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

3. Third parties

The service works with these third parties. Each has its own privacy policy, which governs what it does with the data it receives.

  • Google (Tag Manager and Analytics) — receives the analytics events described above and sets cookies in your browser.
  • Open-Meteo — provides weather forecasts. Forecast requests carry no personal information.
  • OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine, public demo server) — calculates drive routes. When you ask for a drive time, your starting point and the resort's coordinates are sent to OSRM to compute the route.
  • Photon (Komoot) — powers the starting-point search. Text you type in that search box is sent to Photon to suggest places.
  • OpenStreetMap — provides map tiles. Your browser fetches map images directly from OpenStreetMap.
  • Resort and state road sources — including resort websites and snow-report feeds, UDOT, CDOT COtrip, Caltrans, WyoRoad, MDT 511, Idaho 511, NMRoads, 511NY, MassDOT, and Compass. Our server fetches their public reports; your browser does not send them personal information. Links to these sites open their own pages, which are governed by their own policies.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) — embedded live streams load from youtube-nocookie.com.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and delivers the site, including the worker that fetches resort and road data and the storage for cached data and anonymous counts. Requests to the site pass through Cloudflare's network.
  • GitHub — hosts the site's code and configuration, which our worker loads when it starts.

4. How long we keep information

Google Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings. Our anonymous daily counts are aggregate totals by day, with no individual records to keep or delete. Information stored on your device stays there until you clear it in your browser settings. If you email us, we keep your email for as long as needed to answer it.

5. Children's privacy

Follow the Powder is a general-audience service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and there is no part of the service aimed at collecting information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us (for example by email), contact us at admin@followthepowder.com and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

  • Cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, but Google Analytics will not record your visits.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Google offers a browser add-on that stops Google Analytics on any site: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Location: you can decline the browser location request, turn off location permission for this site in your browser settings, or pick an airport as your starting point instead. The site works without a precise location.
  • On-device data: you can clear the site's stored data (favorites, recents, checklist, saved starting point) through your browser's site-data or storage settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the service starts using a new data source. The date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your information, can be sent to admin@followthepowder.com.
